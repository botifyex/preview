# What is new in Mossbill 3

Mossbill is a made-up billing app, and everything in these pictures is sample data. Version 3 makes finding and exporting invoices faster, and it works on a phone. You do not need to do anything.

## Main changes

**Invoices can now be exported.** Export CSV replaces Print list at the top of the invoices page: compare mark 1 on [figure 19](figures/19-before-after-before.png) and [figure 20](figures/20-before-after.png).

**Filters work on one click.** The status filters (mark 2 on [figure 20](figures/20-before-after.png)) replace the drop-down and its Apply button (mark 2 on [figure 19](figures/19-before-after-before.png)). The place where Apply used to be is shown on [figure 7](figures/07-mark-removed.png). Above the filters, [figure 6](figures/06-mark-label.png) shows where to click. Each filter shows how many invoices it holds.

**Add a service in three steps.** Choose a service on Add-ons, check the cart, place the order ([figure 26](figures/26-scheme-checkout-path.png)). The order takes effect at step 3, and the cart is empty again.

**Mossbill on a phone.** The list keeps number, amount and status, and a hint explains swiping, shown only on touch screens ([figure 21](figures/21-mobile-touch.png)). The menu opens from the top left ([figure 22](figures/22-mobile-menu.png)).

**Dark colours.** The app follows your device's dark setting ([figure 18](figures/18-dark-theme.png)).

## Other changes

- Start a new invoice from the dashboard ([figure 1](figures/01-mark-box.png)).
- The bell at the top shows your alerts ([figure 2](figures/02-mark-arrow-default.png)).
- Download a copy of any invoice as a PDF ([figure 3](figures/03-mark-arrow-offset.png)).
- Filtering by Overdue shows each late invoice with its status ([figure 4](figures/04-mark-arrow-from.png)).
- Settings: enter your address, choose your alerts and press Save changes ([figure 5](figures/05-mark-badge.png)).
- Signing in on the demo accepts any password ([figure 8](figures/08-mark-callout.png)).
- Overdue invoices have a red status ([figure 9](figures/09-mark-ellipse.png)).
- Late invoices carry a monthly fee, stated in the payment terms ([figure 10](figures/10-mark-underline.png)).
- Each row of the list gives the number, customer, dates, amount and status of one invoice ([figure 11](figures/11-mark-highlight.png)).
- The dashboard chart shows six months of revenue ([figure 12](figures/12-mark-spotlight.png)).
- Each invoice names who bills and who pays ([figure 13](figures/13-connector-none.png)).
- The late fee applies once the due date has passed ([figure 14](figures/14-connector-end.png)).
- Overdue alerts go to your work email ([figure 15](figures/15-connector-both.png)).
- Monthly statements sit together on one page; this picture is a deliberate exception to one idea per image, there only to compare where numbers can sit ([figure 16](figures/16-number-placements.png)).
- Marks can carry different colours, and a removal stays red ([figure 17](figures/17-colour-levels.png)).
- The list counts its invoices at the very bottom ([figure 23](figures/23-page-whole.png)), which a picture of the window alone does not reach ([figure 24](figures/24-page-window.png)).
- Buttons for reminding and copying appear when the cursor is on a row ([figure 25](figures/25-steps-to-hover.png)).
- An invoice page holds the billed services and the history ([figure 27](figures/27-scheme-invoice-parts.png)).
- The dashboard leads to invoices, and an invoice to settings if you want to change who is notified ([figure 28](figures/28-scheme-screen-map.png)).
- The developer strip is not part of the product and is left out of the pictures ([figure 29](figures/29-scheme-hide-overlay.png)).
